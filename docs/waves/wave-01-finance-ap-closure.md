# Wave 1 closure check: Finance AP (supplier invoices, payments, aging, withholding)

Generated by `validation/checks/carve_wave_slice.py` from `docs/inventories/ssis-sql-lineage.json` and `ssis/orchestration-plan.json`. Static only. Control-framework (`etl.*`) and reject (`err.*`) objects are treated as plumbing and listed separately.

Oracle WWI_FIN accounts-payable subledger and the supplier master it keys on, through raw/stg to Dimension.Supplier, Fact.Supplier Payment, Fact.Supplier Transaction and the month-end AP aging / withholding steps. Fact.Payment is deliberately out of scope: FACT_Load_Payment joins Dimension.Customer and stg.CashReceipt/Lockbox/SEPA, so it is the customer-receipts (AR) fact even though stg.Payment is fed from raw.OracleApPayment. Tax/terms and cost-centre reference extracts are pulled in because the AP staging load depends on them and nothing else owns them as a domain; FX (STG_Load_Currency) is left out as estate-wide plumbing and consumed as a frozen synced input.
